# M&L Link Portfolio

Build a polished, modern, one-page portfolio website for a small digital agency called M&L Link, based in Nairobi, Kenya.

I will provide the following assets separately:
- Logo (to replace the text logo)
- Michelle Miako's photo (female founder)
- Lyeon Kamau's photo (male founder)

Please create placeholders for these so I can easily swap them in later.

---

BRAND POSITIONING

M&L Link is a young digital-solutions agency that helps small and growing businesses establish and improve their digital presence.

We do not want to position ourselves as "just a web design company." Our services include QR-code solutions, websites, digital marketing, digital business cards, digital presence consulting, Google Business Profile setup, and ongoing website care.

The overall feeling should be: Modern • Creative • Trustworthy • Approachable • Young • Professional • Tech-focused

The website should feel like a real boutique digital agency, not a student portfolio or generic SaaS template.

---

BRAND IDENTITY

Brand name: M&L Link

Tagline: Connect your business to the digital world.

Supporting message: Simple digital solutions for growing businesses.

Color palette:
- Primary dark / Deep Navy: #151F4C
- Accent / Muted Olive-Gold: #D2DA97
- Main background / Off-white: #F5F6F8
- Primary text: #10131A
- Secondary text: #5B6472

Use the navy heavily for the hero and footer, off-white for the main content, and Deco sparingly for highlights, icons, small accents, borders, hover states and calls to action.

Do NOT use gradients.
Do NOT use excessive shadows.
Do NOT make the design overly colorful.

---

TYPOGRAPHY

Use:
- Space Grotesk for headings and major display text
- Inter for body text, navigation, buttons and supporting information

Headings should be bold, modern and geometric.
Body text should remain clean and highly readable.

---

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a minimal editorial-style agency portfolio with generous whitespace.

Use:
- Strong typography
- Large headings
- Clean grid layouts
- Thin borders where appropriate
- Small geometric details
- Subtle QR-code-inspired patterns
- Rounded corners only where they improve interactive elements
- Plenty of breathing room between sections

Avoid:
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics
- Huge numbers of cards
- Excessive rounded containers
- Gradients
- Heavy drop shadows
- Stock-photo-heavy layouts
- Overly complicated animations

The design should feel intentional and premium while still being approachable to small-business owners.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must be fully responsive.

Desktop:
- Spacious layouts
- Two-column founder section
- Large typography
- Wide project previews

Tablet:
- Adjust spacing and typography appropriately.

Mobile:
- Single-column layout
- Hamburger navigation
- Comfortable touch targets
- Founder sections stacked vertically
- Services remain easy to scan
- Project images fit the viewport
- No horizontal scrolling
- Hero typography scales cleanly

Make the mobile experience feel intentionally designed, not like a compressed desktop website.
